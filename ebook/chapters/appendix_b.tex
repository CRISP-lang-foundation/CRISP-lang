\texttt{let} & Declaration &
Declares a new variable with optional initialization.\\
& & \texttt{let name = "CRISP";} \hspace{1em} \texttt{let @arr = [1,2];} \\

\texttt{const} & Declaration &
Declares an immutable constant (must be initialized).\\
& & \texttt{const PI = 3.14159;} \\

\texttt{fn} & Function &
Defines a named function with parameters and body.\\
& & \texttt{fn add(a, b) \{ return a + b; \}} \\

\texttt{return} & Control Flow &
Exits a function, optionally returning a value.\\
& & \texttt{return 42;} \hspace{1em} \texttt{return;} (returns null) \\

\texttt{if} & Conditional &
Executes a block if condition is true.\\
& & \texttt{if x > 0 \{ say "positive"; \}} \\

\texttt{else} & Conditional &
Alternative branch for \texttt{if}. Chains with \texttt{if} for \texttt{else if}.\\
& & \texttt{else if x < 0 \{ ... \} else \{ ... \}} \\

\texttt{while} & Loop &
Repeats a block while condition is true.\\
& & \texttt{while i < 10 \{ i = i + 1; \}} \\

\texttt{for} & Loop &
Iterates over arrays, hashes, strings, or ranges.\\
& & \texttt{for item in @arr \{ say item; \}} \\

\texttt{in} & Loop &
Separates loop variable from iterable in \texttt{for} loops.\\
& & \texttt{for ch in "hello" \{ ... \}} \\

\texttt{break} & Loop Control &
Exits the innermost loop immediately. Error outside loops.\\
& & \texttt{if done \{ break; \}} \\

\texttt{continue} & Loop Control &
Skips to the next iteration of the innermost loop. Error outside loops.\\
& & \texttt{if skip \{ continue; \}} \\

\texttt{match} & Pattern Matching &
Branches on value against patterns with optional \texttt{where} guards.\\
& & \texttt{match x \{ 0 => \{ ... \} \_ => \{ ... \} \}} \\

\texttt{where} & Guard &
Adds a condition to a \texttt{match} arm. The arm fires only if guard is true.\\
& & \texttt{n where n > 0 => \{ ... \}} \\

\texttt{try} & Error Handling &
Introduces a block whose errors can be caught.\\
& & \texttt{try \{ risky(); \}} \\

\texttt{catch} & Error Handling &
Catches errors from a preceding \texttt{try} block.\\
& & \texttt{catch e \{ say e; \}} \hspace{1em} \texttt{catch e as err \{ say err; \}} (alias) \\

\texttt{finally} & Error Handling &
Block that always executes after \texttt{try} (and \texttt{catch}), regardless of errors.\\
& & \texttt{finally \{ cleanup(); \}} \\

\texttt{throw} & Error Handling &
Emits a catchable \texttt{UserError} with a message. Interchangeable with \texttt{die}.\\
& & \texttt{throw "invalid input";} \\

\texttt{die} & Error Handling &
Emits a catchable \texttt{UserError} with a message. Interchangeable with \texttt{throw}.\\
& & \texttt{die "fatal error";} \\

\texttt{class} & OOP &
Defines a class with methods and optional parent.\\
& & \texttt{class Point \{ fn new(x, y) \{ ... \} \}} \\

\texttt{extends} & OOP &
Specifies parent class for inheritance.\\
& & \texttt{class Dog extends Animal \{ ... \}} \\

\texttt{self} & OOP &
Refers to the current object instance inside methods.\\
& & \texttt{self.x = 10;} \\

\texttt{super} & OOP &
Calls parent constructor (\texttt{super(args)}) or method (\texttt{super.method(args)}).\\
& & \texttt{super(x, y);} \hspace{1em} \texttt{super.distance();} \\

\texttt{static} & OOP &
Declares a static method (called on class, not instance).\\
& & \texttt{static fn square(x) \{ return x * x; \}} \\

\texttt{new} & OOP (Contextual) &
Contextual keyword for object construction: \texttt{new ClassName(args)}.\\
& & \texttt{let p = new Point(3, 4);} \\

\texttt{print} & Output &
Outputs arguments to stdout without trailing newline.\\
& & \texttt{print "Hello, ", name;} \hspace{1em} \texttt{print("Hello, ", name);} (paren) \\

\texttt{say} & Output &
Outputs arguments to stdout with automatic trailing newline.\\
& & \texttt{say "Hello, ", name, "!";} \hspace{1em} \texttt{say("Hello, ", name);} (paren) \\

\texttt{warn} & Output &
Outputs arguments to stderr with "Warning:" prefix.\\
& & \texttt{warn("low memory");} \\

\texttt{use} & Module &
Loads an opt-in module. Currently \texttt{posix} is the only built-in module.\\
& & \texttt{use posix;} → creates \texttt{posix} hash variable \\

\texttt{true} & Literal &
Boolean true value.\\
& & \texttt{let flag = true;} \\

\texttt{false} & Literal &
Boolean false value.\\
& & \texttt{if done == false \{ ... \}} \\

\texttt{null} & Literal &
Represents absence of value. Returned by failed conversions.\\
& & \texttt{let x = null;} \hspace{1em} \texttt{if x == null \{ ... \}} \\
